Privacy Policy

Last updated June 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which this website collects, uses, maintains, and discloses information collected from users (each, a "User") of this website (the "Site"). This privacy policy applies to the Site and all products and services offered by Emanuel Balmus, a licensed real estate agent with Real Broker LLC operating under The Perry Group.

Personal identification information

We may collect personal identification information from Users in a variety of ways, including but not limited to when Users visit our Site, register on the Site, subscribe to receive a free training, request a consultation, or engage in other activities, services, features, or resources we make available on our Site. Users may be asked to provide, where appropriate, their first name, email address, and phone number.

Providing your phone number is optional. If you choose to provide it, you expressly consent to receive calls and text messages from Emanuel Balmus and The Perry Group at the number provided, including messages sent via automated systems. These communications may include information about your free training, real estate services, appointment reminders, and related follow-up. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may opt out of calls by contacting us directly at the information below.

Users may visit our Site anonymously. We will only collect personal identification information when voluntarily submitted. Users can refuse to supply such information, however it may prevent them from accessing certain Site features or resources.

Non-personal identification information

We may collect non-personal identification information about Users whenever they interact with our Site. This may include browser name, device type, operating system, internet service provider, and other technical information about how Users connect to the Site.

Web browser cookies

Our Site may use 'cookies' to enhance User experience. Users' web browser places cookies on their hard drive for record-keeping purposes and sometimes to track information about them. Users may choose to set their web browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If they do so, note that some parts of the Site may not function properly.

How we use collected information

Emanuel Balmus and The Perry Group may collect and use Users' personal information for the following purposes:

To deliver requested resources. When you opt in to receive the free training, we use your information to provide access to that training and any related materials.

To follow up regarding real estate services. We may use your contact information to follow up about home selling or buying services, schedule consultations, and respond to inquiries.

To improve customer service. Information you provide helps us respond to your requests and support needs more efficiently.

To personalize your experience. We may use information in aggregate to understand how our Users as a group engage with the services and resources we provide.

To send periodic emails and text messages. We may use your email address to send information, updates, and resources related to real estate in Salt Lake County and Utah County. If you provided your phone number, we may also contact you by text or call. All emails include unsubscribe instructions at the bottom. Text messages can be stopped at any time by replying STOP. You may also contact us directly to be removed from any communication list.

How we protect your information

We adopt appropriate data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Sharing your personal information

We do not sell, trade, or rent Users' personal identification information to third parties. We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our Site and conducting our business, provided those parties agree to keep your information confidential. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

Emanuel Balmus is a licensed real estate agent with Real Broker LLC. Real Broker LLC may have its own privacy practices applicable to transactions conducted through the brokerage. We encourage you to review Real Broker LLC's privacy policy separately.

We do not control the content or practices of these websites and are not responsible for their privacy policies. Browsing any third-party website is subject to that website's own terms and policies.
